# Third-party material

Ideas, not text, were taken from the MIT-licensed open-gsd projects (`gsd-core`, `gsd-pi`, `gsd-path`,
`gsd-spec-build-loop`, `gsd-test-runner`): reading commands as argv instead of matching text, verdicts
pinned to a commit, bounded review rounds, verification independent of the builder, and the reviewer's
"guards against nothing" check, which gsd-core in turn adapted from `l-gevity/l-gevity-skills` (MIT). No
file or passage was copied.

Apache-2.0 (`LICENSE-MIT`, `LICENSE-APACHE`).
